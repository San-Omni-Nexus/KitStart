About

DevTools is a beginner-friendly guide to setting up the tools every developer needs. Finding the right software, and getting it installed correctly, can be confusing when you’re just starting out. Each tool on this site comes with a short description, step-by-step setup instructions and a link to the official download. The goal is simple: spend less time fighting your setup and more time writing code.
